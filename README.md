# 📊 Sales Analyzer using Core Python

A **Sales Analyzer** project built using **Core Python** to analyze sales data, calculate important business metrics, answer sales-related questions, and export filtered data into CSV files.

This project focuses on implementing data analysis concepts using Python without relying on advanced data-analysis libraries such as Pandas.

---

## 🚀 Project Overview

The **Sales Analyzer** reads sales data from a CSV file and performs various operations to understand sales performance.

The project can calculate:

* Total revenue
* Total number of orders
* Total quantity of items sold
* Revenue by category
* Revenue by product
* Best-selling product by quantity
* Highest-spending customer
* Revenue by date
* Revenue between two given dates
* Export orders from a specific category into a new CSV file

## 📋 Business Questions Answered

### 1. What is the Total Revenue for all Sales?

Calculates the total revenue generated from all orders.

**Formula:**

```text
Total Revenue = Sum of Revenue from all orders
```

---

### 2. How many Total Orders were Placed?

Counts the total number of orders present in the sales dataset.

---

### 3. How many Total Items (Quantity) were Sold?

Calculates the total quantity of products sold across all orders.

**Formula:**

```text
Total Items Sold = Sum of Quantity
```

---

### 4. What is the Total Revenue Generated by Each Category?

Groups sales according to product category and calculates the total revenue generated by each category.

Example:

```text
Electronics → ₹XX,XXX
Clothing   → ₹XX,XXX
Furniture  → ₹XX,XXX
```

---

### 5. What is the Total Revenue Generated by Each Product?

Calculates the total revenue generated by every individual product.

This helps identify which products contribute the most revenue.

---

### 6. Which Product has the Highest Quantity Sold?

Identifies the **best-selling product based on quantity**.

The product with the highest total quantity sold is returned as the answer.

---

### 7. Which Customer Spent the Most Money?

Calculates the total amount spent by each customer and identifies the customer with the highest total spending.

---

### 8. What is the Total Revenue for Each Date?

Groups the sales according to their order date and calculates the total revenue generated on each date.

This helps analyze daily sales performance.

---

### 9. What is the Total Revenue Between Two Given Dates?

Allows the user to provide a start date and an end date and calculates the total revenue generated during that period.

Example:

```text
Start Date: 2026-01-01
End Date:   2026-01-31
```

The program returns the total revenue generated between these dates.

---

### 10. Export All Orders Under the "Electronics" Category into a New CSV File

Filters all orders belonging to the **Electronics** category and exports them into a separate CSV file.

Example output:

```text
output/electronics_orders.csv
```
